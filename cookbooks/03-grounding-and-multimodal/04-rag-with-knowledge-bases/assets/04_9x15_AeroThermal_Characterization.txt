                           Calibration

 Static pressure, total pressure, and total temperature calibration
  relationships were generated using data collected with the 15-foot
  traversing calibration rake.
 Static pressure decreases axially though the test section due to
  boundary layer growth causing reduced flow area and increased
  Mach number near.
 Total temperature data agrees with historical trends and
  continues to show increased total temperature near the south
  wall. The total temperature calibration captures this change.

7
               Construction Acceptance Criteria

 Prior to the facility modifications a series of criteria were agreed
  upon to ensure the test section flow quality was not significantly
  reduced.
 The criteria evaluated the changes to spatial flow uniformity, flow
  angularity, and turbulence intensity by comparing full
  characterization measurement to measurements collected prior to
  the facility modifications. No reduction of the Mach number
  operating envelope was allowed.
 All construction acceptance criteria were successfully accepted.

8
                                 Conclusion

 Following facility modifications a full characterization test entry
  was successfully completed. During the test entry:
    •   Flow field surveys were conducted.
    •   Boundary layer surveys were conducted.
    •   Calibration relationships were generated.
    •   Construction acceptance criteria were met.
