13.     ABSTRACT        (Maximum       200   words)

        This paper summarizes     a variety of optically based flow-visualization    techniques used for high-speed research by
        the Configuration   Aerodynamics    Wind-Tunnel-Test   Team of the High-Speed        Research Program during its tenure.
        The work of other national experts is included for completeness.        Details of each technique with applications  and
        status in various national wind tunnels are given.
